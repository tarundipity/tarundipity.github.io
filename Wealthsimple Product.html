Wealthsimple — the Student Starter Platform

The challenge

For the Wealthsimple Live Case Competition in BU111, our team was asked to propose a way for Wealthsimple to win over a notoriously tricky audience: students. Gen Z is curious about investing but often intimidated by it — and expensive to acquire as customers. We needed an idea that built genuine financial confidence and made business sense for Wealthsimple.

01

The problem

Students want to start investing but lack the confidence and literacy to begin — and traditional acquisition (ads, incentives) is costly with low long-term retention.

02

Our proposal

A Student Starter Platform built into the Wealthsimple app — a low-stakes place to learn by doing, with perks that reward students for sticking around.

the platform

What we built into it

📈

Simulated trading

A risk-free investing sandbox so students can learn the mechanics before using real money.

📚

Bite-size education

Condensed, student-friendly modules that make financial concepts approachable.

🎁

Student perks

Exclusive discounts and rewards through WS Cash and the WS Card to drive everyday use.

🎓

On-campus rollout

A targeted campus marketing plan to drive adoption at a low acquisition cost.
